About Me

Hello! My name is Olivia Mojica. This portfolio website showcases my range of abilities in my design career, from typography and graphic design to more extensive multi-step projects and personal work, including my interest in collage and political activism. I don't want my work just to be laid out on the page; I want it to be experienced and appreciated for its content and effort. At the forefront, I want to spotlight my work, the identity and values behind it, and myself. However, I still want anyone viewing the site to be enticed enough at the forefront to stay.
